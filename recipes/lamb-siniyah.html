Lamb Siniyah

Serves 10 · 1 serving per container Per serving ~720 kcal · 73 g protein · 21 g total carbs · 40 g fat Active 35 min Total ~2½ hr Equipment 8 L casserole pot · 2 baking dishes · Steamer · Masher · 10 shallow freezer containers
12 × 250 gLamspouletIers lamsvlees · thaw in fridge, then pat dry
1 tspOlive oil

Brown

Heat oil over medium-high. Brown about one-third of the lamb in batches. Set browned and raw lamb aside separately.

1 × 500 gYellow onionsGourmet Gele uien bio
1 × 350 gCeleryCrisp Bleekselderij bio

Dice

Peel the onions and trim the celery. Dice both finely.

2 tbspBaharator 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp each cumin and coriander, ½ tsp each cinnamon and allspice

To tasteSalt and black pepper
As neededWater

Braise

Soften vegetables in the pot with a splash of water, 10 min. Stir in baharat, then all the lamb, tomatoes and seasoning.

Cover and simmer gently until fork tender. Add water if it sticks. Uncover at the end if the sauce needs thickening.

75–100 min simmering

Toast

Shake in a dry skillet over medium heat until golden.

2–4 min

Whisk

Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic and salt. Gradually add water until smooth and pourable.

Bake

Stir nuts and parsley into the braise. Divide between two baking dishes and pour tahini evenly over both.

Bake at 180 °C fan, uncovered, until the tahini sets and browns lightly.

25–35 min
2 × 400 gCauliflower floretsHessing Bloemkoolroosjes
As neededWater

Steam

Steam florets until very soft. Drain well.

12–15 min
To tasteSalt

Mash

Mash cauliflower with salt. Leave it a little coarse.

Portion

Divide the mash between 10 shallow containers. Top each with one-fifth of the lamb and crust from one baking dish.

Freeze

Chill promptly in the fridge, then seal and freeze. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat, covered, to 74 °C throughout.
