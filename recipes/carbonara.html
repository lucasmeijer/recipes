Spaghetti Carbonara

Serves 4 Active 20 min Total 25 min Equipment Large pot · 12″ skillet · Mixing bowl · Tongs · Microplane
4 LWater
1 TbspKosher salt

Boil

Cover and bring to a rolling boil.

~10 min
400 gSpaghettibronze-cut if you can

Cook

Add pasta, stir. Cook 2 min less than the package says.

8 min
150 gGuancialeor thick pancetta

Cut

Into 1 cm lardons, rind removed.

Render

Start in a cold, dry skillet → medium, so fat renders before the meat browns. Stir until crisp. Keep the fat.

8–10 min

Toss

Tong pasta into skillet with ½ cup pasta water. Toss on medium until glossy. Scoop out 1 more cup of water.

~90 s
4Egg yolks
1Whole egg
50 gPecorino Romanofinely grated
25 gParmigianofinely grated
2 tspBlack peppercoarsely cracked

Whisk

Yolks, egg, both cheeses and pepper into a thick paste. Leave by the stove.

Emulsify

Heat OFF, wait 30 s (eggs scramble above ~80 °C). Add egg paste and toss hard, adding pasta water a spoonful at a time until silky. Thickens as it cools.

Serve

Immediately, into warm bowls. Top with extra pecorino and pepper.
